# Writing for people

Docs, docstrings and comments here are read by people. Follow
`.agents/writing_style.md`. The rules that come up most:

- American spelling; "data sheet" as two words.
- Define a term or symbol on first use, or link `docs/glossary.md` /
  `docs/symbols.md`, and add new ones there.
- Docstrings are one or two paragraphs; point to `docs/` or a data sheet
  page for detail.
- Comments only explain what the code cannot. No step labels, review IDs or
  notes to agents.
